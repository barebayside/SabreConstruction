Should you move,
or rebuild?

The honest answer, from a Capalaba family builder who's been doing it since 1990.

It's not the house. It's the year.

You've outgrown the house. You haven't outgrown the street. Moving means a new school run and a mortgage set by whatever the market's doing this month. Staying means living through a build.

Nobody lies awake over cornice profiles. They lie awake over the twelve months in between — and over the three things nobody brings up until after you've signed.

Where will the kids live?

Most families rent nearby to stay in the catchment. It's the single biggest cost people forget, so we put it on the table at the site visit — before anything is signed. If renting for nine months breaks the numbers, better to know now than halfway through.

Will the price move?

You get an itemised list of inclusions and a fixed price before you commit. Change your mind later — most families do on something — and we price the change and show you what it does to the total before we touch it.

Will anyone pick up?

Frank has run our sites for seventeen years. Celene is in the office. Same two people from slab to handover, and you'll know what's happening this week without chasing it.

Who you'd be dealing with.

Stewart Brain picked up the tools at seventeen and started Sabre with his wife Cathy in 1990. Thirty-six years later he's still the one who comes out and walks your block. Cathy has run the business side since the beginning, and Nathan — the middle of their three kids — is on the tools now.

They built the company and raised a family at the same time. That's the reason it's set up the way it is.

What eleven people said.
