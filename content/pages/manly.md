# Stay in Manly. Build the house that fits.

Families in Manly don't leave Manly lightly. You're near the water, the kids are settled, and the
walk to the harbour is the reason you're there in the first place.

The house is the part that can change. A knockdown rebuild replaces it and keeps everything else —
the address, the neighbours, the school, the ten-minute walk that made you buy the place.

## What a Manly block is really worth designing around

Most houses in Manly were built before anyone thought about where the light comes from.

That's the opportunity. On a Bayside block, the difference between a house that's pleasant in
February and a house that isn't is mostly free at the drawing stage and impossible afterwards.
Which rooms face the breeze. Where the living areas sit. Whether you can see any water from
anywhere, and if you can, whether the house is arranged to make use of it. Where the afternoon sun
lands in summer, and whether that's a good thing or the reason the back room is unusable.

An older house on a good Manly block often has all of that the wrong way round, because it was
laid out to face the street and nothing else. A new house on the same block doesn't have to be.

## Can we knock it down in Manly?

Manly has streets of older houses near the harbour, and Brisbane City Council protects the look of
some of them through the **Traditional building character overlay**.

If your house was built in 1946 or earlier and your street sits in the overlay, demolition needs a
development application and council decides — not automatically no, not automatically yes. Built
after 1946, and the character rules almost certainly aren't your problem. Built before 1911, and
council's position is that the house stays.

It's mapped street by street, so the house two doors down tells you nothing reliable. Checking your
own address is free and takes about five minutes:

- **[Can we knock down our house? Brisbane's rules in plain words](/blog/can-we-knock-down-our-house/)**
- **[How to check if your house is in the character overlay](/blog/check-if-your-street-is-in-the-character-overlay/)**

## Common questions about building in Manly

**We're close to the water. Does that change anything?**
It can. Coastal blocks sometimes carry overlays of their own on top of the character one, and
they're on the same council map. It's part of the same free check, and it's better known at the
start than discovered at the drawing stage.

**Can we build two storeys?**
Often, and what decides it is the block and the planning rules for your specific address rather
than the suburb. It's one of the things worth asking on the walk-through.

**Where do we live while it's built?**
Not in the house — for a full rebuild there isn't one. Most families rent nearby so the kids stay
in the same school and plan the build around the school year. It's the biggest cost people forget,
which is why Nathan raises it early rather than late.
