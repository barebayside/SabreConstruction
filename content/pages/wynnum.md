# Stay in Wynnum. Build the house that fits.

You didn't buy the house. You bought the street, the water at the end of it, and the school the
kids walk to. When the house runs out of room, moving away isn't the only answer on the table.

A knockdown rebuild keeps the address and replaces the house. Same street, same neighbours, same
school run — a home built around your family instead of around a family from sixty years ago.

## What we see on Wynnum blocks

Wynnum is one of the Bayside's older suburbs, and that cuts both ways.

The good part is the land. Wynnum blocks were laid out when land wasn't scarce, and a lot of them
will comfortably take a modern family home with room left over — which is exactly the thing that
can't be bought back once a suburb has been subdivided.

The complicated part is the age of the houses. There are a lot of pre-war homes in Wynnum, and
Brisbane City Council protects the look of some older streets through the **Traditional building
character overlay**. It's mapped street by street, so one side of a road can be in it and the
other side not. It is the first thing to check about any Wynnum address, before a design, before a
budget, before anything.

## Can we knock it down in Wynnum?

Maybe, and it's checkable for free in about five minutes.

If your house was built in 1946 or earlier and your street sits in the character overlay,
demolition needs a development application and council decides. Not automatically no, not
automatically yes. If it went up after 1946, the character rules almost certainly aren't your
issue. If it was built before 1911, council's position is that the house is kept.

## Common questions about building in Wynnum

**Our house is pre-war. Is that the end of it?**
No. It means council decides, on an application, and there are limited situations where a pre-1947
house can come down. It's worth finding out properly rather than assuming either way.

**Where do we live while it's built?**
Not in the house — for a full rebuild there isn't one. Most Wynnum families rent nearby so the
kids stay in the same school, and plan the build around the school year. It's the biggest cost
people forget, so Nathan raises it on the walk-through rather than after you've signed.

**Do you build in Wynnum West and Lota too?**
Yes — Wynnum, Wynnum West, Manly, Manly West, Lota, Hemmant, Tingalpa, Wakerley, Capalaba,
Birkdale, Wellington Point, Cleveland and across the Bayside and Redlands.

**Do we need plans before we call?**
