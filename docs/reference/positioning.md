# Positioning — the one difference Sabre is sold on

> **Layer 4. Set by Edward 2026-09-08, in conversation.** This is the marketing spine for
> every Sabre page, ad and post from now on. It sits above `copy-rules.md` (voice) and
> beside the campaign plan §3 (the postcode positioning). Where they differ, this wins.

## The difference

**Nathan is your builder, on call, with you every step of the way.** He only takes on a
certain number of projects at a time, so the family that books him gets him the whole way
through, from the first walk through their old house to the day they get the keys. Sabre
has been doing this since 1990, and the reputation they've built is exactly that: they
stay with you.

Everything on the site is family-oriented: it is a family building a family's home, with a
family builder.

## The path (the "problem" section on the home page)

The problem, in one line: you've got something old, you want something new, how do you go
about it? The answer is three steps, and nothing else on that page:

1. **Nathan walks your house with you.**
2. **We help you with the designers.** The design comes from an architect Sabre works with.
   Keep it that simple.
3. **Then we build it.** Your new family home. And we're with you every step of the way.

## What this changes

- **The front door is a conversation, not a block check.** The primary button becomes the
  walk-through with Nathan. The free block check stays on the contact page for people who
  arrive already thinking about a rebuild.
- **Knockdown-rebuild is the target market but not the opening line.** It comes after other
  conversations. "Can we knock it down?" moves off the home page's closing band.
- **Problems are only raised where they're answered.** "Do we have to move out?" is not on the
  home page. It becomes one properly answered question on the "Can we knock it down?" page.
- **The Designs page is examples of what Sabre has built**, not a catalogue to start from.
  The path is designer-led.
