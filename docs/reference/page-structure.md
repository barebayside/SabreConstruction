# Page structure — and why each section sits where it does

> **Layer 4.** Canonical source for section order. Change the order only with a
> reason that beats the one written here.

## The method

Every section is judged against the five tests from
`BBL/playbook/foundations.md` (see `bbl-links.md`). A cold paid click has to win all five:

| Test | Their question |
|---|---|
| **Notice** | Is this worth my attention? |
| **Fit** | Is this about me? |
| **Proof** | Is it true? |
| **Effort** | What does finding out cost me? |
| **Now** | Why not later? |

A section that answers no test is decoration. Cut it or repurpose it.

## `index.html` — the main site page

| # | Section | Test | Why here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero (still image) | Notice + Fit | Stops them and names them. Three trust badges do above-the-fold credibility work |
| 2 | Suburb strip | Fit | Two seconds of "my area". Makes outsiders self-exclude |
| 3 | Transformation video | Notice + Proof | Pays off whatever the ad promised. Only place the video plays |
| 4 | Testimonials + written quote | Proof | Named video testimonials sit near the top of the trust hierarchy |
| 5 | Gallery (lightbox) | Proof | Real finished houses. Harder proof than words |
| 6 | Credentials strip | Proof | The checkable stuff — 1990, licence, 36 years |
| 7 | Meet the family | Liking + Unity | "Who are you to promise that?" |
| 8 | What we sort out | Effort | Three one-line worries, then four steps |
| 9 | FAQ | Effort | Last objections before the ask |
| 10 | CTA + form | Effort + Now | The close |

**Trust is fully delivered by section 6.** That's the point of this order —
research says users spend ~57% of viewing time above the fold, so the strongest
proof cannot wait until section 9.

### Things deliberately removed

- **The dark problem band.** Three paragraphs in a dark box. Nobody reads that at
  glancing speed. The headline survived; the fears are one line each now.
- **The duplicate video.** The hero and the section below it were playing the
  same file. Hero is a still now.
- **"25+ years", "100% family owned", "1 point of contact".** Rounded-down or
  slogans wearing a number's clothes.

## The three landing pages — one per medium

| Page | Medium | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `lp-rebuild.html` | Meta Reels & Stories | 9:16. Photo, headline, form. Nothing else above the fold |
| `lp-raise.html` | Meta Feed | 1:1 / 4:5. Video, three numbers, form, what happens next |
| `lp-story.html` | Google Ads | Desktop 16:9. Two columns — answers left, form sticky right |

Each prints its medium as a pill under the header so a reviewer can see what the
page is a destination for.

**The form is embedded on all three**, open on question one. Someone arriving
from an ad has already opted in by tapping it; a second click only loses people.

## Two standing rules

**No fabricated scarcity.** The "Now" test is carried by honest loss framing —
the school term you're losing — never a countdown or a fake cap. A cap you'd
cave on is a fabrication that happens to be about you rather than the market.

**Concrete over rounded.** "Since 1990" beats "25+ years". A checkable specific
beats an adjective every time.
