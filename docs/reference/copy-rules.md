# Copy rules

> **Layer 4.** Voice and claim rules. Facts themselves live in
> `client-facts.md`. Method lives in `BBL/playbook/foundations.md` — see
> `bbl-links.md`.

## Who we're talking to

A family who loves their street, has outgrown the house on it, and is weighing
up moving against rebuilding. Two kids, a school catchment, a mortgage.

Test every line: **would someone outside that group self-exclude?** If a
developer, a retiree and a young family all read the same sentence comfortably,
it's aimed at nobody.

## Voice

- Plain Australian. Contractions. Short sentences.
- Say the thing, then stop. No throat-clearing, no "we pride ourselves on".
- Second person. "Your block", "your kids", not "our clients".
- Concrete beats adjective. "Since 1990" not "long-established". "About an
  hour" not "a quick chat".
- Name the fear in their words, not the industry's. "Where do the kids live?"
  not "accommodation during construction".

## What the copy must never do

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| Invent a number, rating, or review count | See `client-facts.md`. There is no verifiable Google rating |
| Use a countdown, cap or deadline | Scarcity must be enforceable or it's a fabrication about yourself |
| Present an AI visualisation as a finished job | The raise reels land on a generated house. They need a caption |
| Present the AI testimonials as customers | Three guards on those, all stay |
| Round a real number down | Their site says 25+ years. It's 36 |
| Promise on Sabre's behalf without confirmation | The fixed-price and one-point-of-contact claims are ours, not theirs. Flagged in `../open-items.md` |

## Honest urgency

The "Now" test is real here without inventing anything: build timelines run
against school years, and every term spent waiting is another term in a house
that doesn't fit. That's loss framing on something true.

## Headline patterns that are working

- **"Same street. Same school. A whole new house."** — Fit and the whole offer
  in eight words.
- **"It's not the house. It's the year."** — reframes the objection.
- **"Don't move. Go up."** — the raise angle in four words.
- **"Old house in. New home out."** — the transformation, with an underline
  under the payoff. Not a strike-through; that reads as crossing it out.

## Length

People glance. Three paragraphs in a dark box gets read by nobody. If a section
can be three lines instead of three hundred words, make it three lines — that
was the single biggest improvement to the main page.
