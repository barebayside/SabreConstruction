Hi Nathan,

A few questions for a voice note, whenever suits: in the ute, on a smoko break, whatever works. Talk the way you'd talk to a client standing on their block. There are no wrong answers, and "I don't know" or "it depends" is fine. Just tell us what it depends on.

Around 20–30 minutes total. Skip anything you'd rather not answer. Please don't name clients or give street addresses; "a job in Manly last year" is perfect.

**A. The walk-through (the first visit)**
1. When a family asks you to come out, what actually happens? Who comes, how long do you spend, and what do you do?
2. When you walk through an old house, what do you look at first?
3. What tells you straight away that a rebuild makes sense? And what tells you it doesn't, that they'd be better off renovating or staying put?
4. What should a family have ready before you come? Plans, a survey, a rough budget, a list of what they want?
5. Is it you who does the walk-through, or Stewart, or both?

**B. How long it takes, and where the family lives**
6. From the first visit to handing over the keys, what are the big stages, roughly in order?
7. Roughly how long does each stage take on a typical job? Ranges are fine. What makes one job quicker or slower than another?
8. Where do your families usually live during the build? Anything you tell them to sort out early?
9. Demolition: who organises it, you or the family? How long does it usually take once the power's been disconnected?

**C. The design**
10. Who draws the new house: an architect, a building designer, a draftsperson, or your own designs? Can we name who you work with?
11. How do your own designs (Killarney, Clarence and the rest) fit in? Do people pick one and change it, or start from scratch?

**D. Cost (no numbers needed, just what you've seen)**
12. What most often surprises a family once the old house is opened up or pulled down? One real example, with no names or address.
13. What's the one thing families spend on and never regret? And the one thing they save on and wish they hadn't?

**E. The suburbs**
14. What do you notice about Wynnum blocks: slope, soil, access, the old houses on them?
15. Same for Manly: the hill, the harbour side, access, anything that catches people out?
16. A Sabre home in or near Wynnum or Manly we could mention, by design name or street name only, and one true thing about it?

**F. How Sabre works (quick yes or no, and a sentence if you like)**
17. Are you the family's contact the whole way through, start to finish?
18. How many homes do you take on at a time? A number, or tell us how you'd put it.
19. Which contract do you use: QBCC, HIA, Master Builders, or your own? Usually fixed price?
20. Are these true of how Sabre works? "Fixed price before you commit." "One point of contact." "We'll tell you if it's not the right time to build." "The first site visit is free."

**G. Anything else**
21. What's a question families should ask a builder but almost never do?
22. What's something you wish every family knew before they started?
23. Anything else you think the website should say?

Thanks mate, this is what turns the website from "a builder" into Sabre.
