Fair questions

Is my house allowed to come down?

If it was built after 1946, Brisbane's character rules almost certainly don't stop you. If it's older and your street is in the character overlay, council decides on an application. We check the map with you first.

Where do we live while you build?

Most families rent nearby to stay in the school catchment. It's the biggest cost people forget, so it's on the table at the first conversation, not after you've signed.

Do we need our own plans?

No. Start from one of six Sabre designs and change what you like, or bring your own architect's drawings. Sabre builds both ways.
