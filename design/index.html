Sabre Constructions · design options · 2026-09-08

Three homepages. Same words. Pick the skin.

All three use what is printed on Sabre's own signage: black #0A0A0A, red #EB2227, white, and Arial Bold. All three carry the same conversion spine from the BBL copy method: hero with one call to action → checkable proof → the problem in their words → three benefits → four steps → real reviews → fair questions → the free block check. Only the look changes.

The hero on every option is a video slot. On phones it plays Nathan's own clip from a real Sabre site. On desktop it plays a wide crop of the same clip until the drone flyover exists.

Option A

The Hedger read. Dark-first. Full-bleed video, then calm alternating black and white bands, image one side and words the other, a short red rule above each heading. Ends on a red band.

Option B

The Yama read. Light, editorial, zoomed out. Small headline above the video, big air, a ruled three-number row, the six designs as a showcase, one dark mosaic, one wide photo with a line on it.

Option C

The signage. The fence banner turned into a website. Black ground, red diagonal bands between sections, one red word per headline, a red service stripe, the loudest of the three.

Every number on these pages is in site/docs/client-facts.md with a source. The video is real footage, not AI. Nothing here is on the live site until Nathan chooses and design-system.md is rewritten.
