Check first: is every line true?

Nathan says these as fact, on camera. If he says "that's not quite how we do it", write his words in the box and change the line before filming. Never film a line he isn't sure of.

"I come out and walk through your house with you"Is it Nathan who does the walk-through (not Stewart)?
"We're with you every step of the way" / "the same person answering the phone"Is that really how a build runs?
"We only take on a handful of homes at a time"True? No number unless he gives one.
"We help you with the designers"Who are they? Fine to keep it vague on camera.
Extra line, only if true: "Custom doesn't have to mean it costs more"A price claim: it has to be true (consumer law). If yes, what's his reason?
The exact name the QBCC licence is held inGoes on screen on every ad, with QBCC 15486557.
⛔ Not in the scripts: "I'm the owner." The owners on record are Stewart and Cathy, so the scripts say "Mum and Dad started Sabre back in 1990", which is true.

How to record

  • Stand, or sit on a stool. A chair with a back stays sharp behind him and looks odd.
  • Look at the camera lens, not the screen.
  • One block at a time. Pause a breath between lines, so they cut cleanly.
  • Each hook on its own, three times. Hooks are what we test in ads, so we want plenty of them.
  • Two takes of each body. If he wants to say it his own way, let him. His words beat these.
  • Tick each line as it's done (the tick buttons save on this page).

1. Introduction

about 45 secondssite video · start of longer videos · cold-audience ad
Hi, I'm Nathan from Sabre Constructions.
We're a family building company. Mum and Dad started Sabre back in 1990, and we've been building on the Bayside ever since.
A lot of the families we talk to are in the same spot.
They love where they live. The street, the school, the neighbours.
But the house just doesn't fit them anymore.
So instead of selling up and moving away, we knock the old house down and build a new one, right there on the same block.
The way it works is pretty simple.
First, I come out and walk through your house with you. What's working, what's not, and what your family needs.
Then we help you with the designers, so the new house is drawn around your family and your block.
Then we build it. And we're with you every step of the way.
If that sounds like you, book a walk-through. I'll give you a call and we'll find a time that suits.

2. Angle A: Stay on the street you love

about 30 seconds
Don't move out of the area to get more room. Upgrade the house, keep the life.

Hooks: record each one on its own

Love your street, but the house is too small?
You don't have to leave the area to get a bigger house.
Same street. Same school. A whole new house.

Body

Most families we meet aren't looking to move.
The kids are at the local school. Their mates are just down the road.
The problem's the house. Not enough bedrooms, one bathroom for everyone, a kitchen nobody fits in.
A knockdown rebuild means you keep all the good stuff. The street, the school, the neighbours.
You just end up with a house that actually fits your family.
Book a walk-through and I'll come and have a look.

3. Angle B: Custom means built around you

about 30 seconds
"Custom" doesn't mean fancy. It means the house is worked out around the family.

Hooks: record each one on its own

Custom home doesn't mean a fancy home.
When we say custom, we mean built around your family.
Why squeeze your family into a plan made for someone else?

Body

When people hear "custom builder", they think big and fancy.
That's not what it means to us.
It means the house is worked out around your family. How many kids. Where they sleep. Where dinner happens. Where all the stuff goes.
Not a plan off a shelf you have to fit your family into.
It starts with me walking through your house with you, room by room, and you telling me what's stopped working.
Then the designers draw it around your family and your block.
And custom doesn't have to mean it costs more.
Book a walk-through and we'll start there.
The "costs more" line only goes in if Nathan ticks it above. Real proof for the edit, on screen: Lesley W's review, "The first thing Stewart pointed out was the plans could be changed to build it cheaper."

4. Angle C: One builder, the whole way

about 30 seconds
A family builder who stays with you, not a company with a sales team and a hand-off.

Hooks: record each one on its own

The builder who walks your house should be the one who hands you the keys.
We only take on a handful of homes at a time. Here's why.
We're a family, building family homes.

Body

We're a family business. Mum and Dad started Sabre in 1990.
We only take on a handful of homes at a time, and that's on purpose.
It means the person who walks through your old house with you is the same person answering the phone the whole way through. Right up until we hand you the keys.
No sales team. No hand-off.
Thirty-six years on the Bayside, and that's what people say about us. We stay with you.
Book a walk-through and you'll see what I mean.
