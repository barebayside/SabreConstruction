Who it's for

The customer card. These are still guesses until three real buyers have told us.

Who

Young families who already own an older, cheaper Bayside house and have run out of room. They have some equity.

What sets it off

Another child, parents moving in, or a repair quote that frightened them.

What they want

"A Family Home", not just a house. Same street, same school.

What nearly stops them

Hidden costs, the number moving, a builder who disappears, and whether the house is even allowed to come down.

We are here · waiting on Nathan's numbers

Build the offer

"Your 2027 family rebuild: demolition included, plus the [name] upgrade pack worth $[X]. Only [N] families, sign by [date]."

In the offer

  • ✅ Demolition included
  • ✅ Upgrade pack (a bonus with a stated value, never a discount)
  • ❓ Early costs covered: soil test, survey, council checks
  • ✅ The walk-through stays free

Left out (your call)

  • ❌ Guarantees
  • ❌ Weekly videos
  • ❌ Family moments

"That's literally what builders do."

Owed by Nathan today

  • How many homes in 2027
  • Demolition cost + who gets it
  • Pack contents, retail value, cost to Sabre
  • Early costs: amount + who's covered
  • Latest sign-by date
  • Rough profit per build
  • Monthly ad budget

Then: the 30-day check. Does one build's profit still pay for the demolition and pack, plus the ad spend?

Rules that always apply

  • No invented numbers: no prices, timelines or job counts unless Nathan gives them
  • The rating is 4.5 from 12. Never 5
  • AI-generated people are never shown as customers
  • No generated "after" house, ever
  • Ads never land on the github.io preview, only the real domain
