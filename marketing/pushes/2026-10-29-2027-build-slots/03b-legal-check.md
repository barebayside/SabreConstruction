## Push C (warm / referrals) — watch these
- **Referral fees to brokers or architects:** paying a professional to send their client to Sabre, without the client knowing, can be a **secret commission** offence (Qld Criminal Code Ch 42A, giver and receiver both liable). Rule: disclosed to the client in writing, or none. A thank-you to a past client is fine.
- **Reviews:** ask EVERY finished client, not only the happy ones. Any incentive must be disclosed and given whatever they write. (Same applies to BBL's gated review survey: check before using it for Sabre.)
- **Email / SMS to past clients:** need consent (express is safest), sender ID, unsubscribe. Letterbox drops to site neighbours: no Spam Act issue.

## Coming
- **Unfair trading practices law** passed 2 July 2026, **starts 1 July 2027** (inside the 2027 build year). Targets pressure tactics and manipulation. A real dated offer is fine; fake countdowns or "reserve now or lose it" pushes are the risk.
