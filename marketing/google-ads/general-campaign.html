What the research found

  • Very little paid competition in the pocket. Only 7 of 58 searches showed any ad, all on "builders / home builders <suburb>" (Gumdale, Birkdale, Tingalpa, Murarrie, Manly West, Ransome), all volume builders (Orbit Homes, Meadan Homes, Plantation Homes). No knockdown rebuild search showed an ad at all.
  • Nobody types "knockdown rebuild" + suburb. Google only suggests it at Brisbane level ("knock down rebuild builders brisbane", "… cost", "… prices"). So the clever play is Brisbane-wide words, shown only to people in the 13 suburbs.
  • The long tail is "builders <suburb>": Google's related searches are buyers ("small builders wynnum qld", "independent home builders wynnum qld", "builders wynnum qld reviews", "best builders manly qld").
  • Cost is the biggest question ("knock down rebuild brisbane cost" and "… cost calculator" top the related searches; the People-also-ask boxes are mostly price). We answer with the cost article, never a number.
  • Sabre is in none of the top 10 results (expected: the new site is hidden until the domain moves). The organic results are mostly realestate.com.au, hipages, Facebook and volume builders.
  • Sabre is in none of the map listings for "home builder" around Wynnum, Manly, Capalaba, Birkdale, Gumdale or Hemmant (49 listings). Wynnum, Manly and Birkdale are held by small builders with 0–11 reviews (Saltash Homes 5.0 from 8, NLH 5.0 from 4, BTB and Tierney with none), so a fixed profile with Sabre's 12 reviews can compete there. Capalaba is crowded: Fiteni 4.8 from 69, CJ Homes 4.3 from 75, Lindon 4.8 from 19, Dixon 4.6 from 35. The map box follows the address, so the Ransome fix matters.
  • ⚠️ One sample, desktop, not located in the suburbs. Google shows different ads to someone sitting in Wynnum, and this gives no search volumes or click prices. On build day, run Keyword Planner inside Sabre's account for real volumes and bids before turning anything on.
