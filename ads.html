Concept 01 · Ready to run

The Rebuild

"Same street. Same school.
A whole new house."

A tired old lowset comes down and a family home assembles itself in its place. It stops the scroll because nobody expects a house to go up in ten seconds — and the headline answers the question a family is actually asking before they've finished watching.

  • 0–2sOld lowset. Headline already on screen.
  • 2–5sIt comes down. Dust.
  • 5–13sNew home assembles piece by piece. The retention driver.
  • 13–16sFinished home holds. CTA and licence on screen.
How it's made Generated as two segments — demolition, then assembly — each with fixed start and end frames, so the final frame is Sabre's real photograph rather than an invented house. The master is 2.38:1 because it was built from that wide photo, so rather than crop three quarters of it away, the vertical cut puts the headline and CTA in the space above and below.
9:16 · Reels — real footage
Concept 02 · Ready to run · 3 cuts

The Raise

"This is what your place
looks like in nine months."

Your own footage of the raise-and-build-under carries the first half — the steel stumps, the temporary fencing, the real mess of a working site. Then the frame completes itself into a finished Hamptons Queenslander with a family walking up the path and the dog on the lawn. The authenticity of the first half is what makes the second half land.

  • 0–4sReal site. Real audio — wind, traffic. This is the trust.
  • 4–5sThe frame starts to complete. Fencing lifts away.
  • 5–10sFinished home, landscaped, warm light. Family walks in.
  • 10–11sHolds on the family. CTA card.
How it's made The last frame of your real clip becomes the AI's first frame, so the join is invisible — the generated half literally starts on the pixels the camera stopped on. Your footage is never touched or restyled.

Before this runs The finished house is an artist's impression — there's no design or render for this job yet, so the second half is a visualisation on top of a real, identifiable site. It needs "concept visualisation" on screen, or we re-cut it to land on one of Sabre's genuinely finished homes.
16:9 · Feed & retargeting
Concept 03 · Needs real customers

The Straight Answer

"I never had to chase anyone."

A past client, filmed plainly, saying one specific thing. Not a highlight reel — one sentence about one worry they had. This is the retargeting ad: it runs at families who already visited the page and didn't book.

  • 0–3sStraight into the sentence. No intro, no logo sting.
  • 3–8sThey expand on it in their own words.
  • 8–12sCut to the finished house. Name and suburb on screen.
How it's made The clip shown here is an AI stand-in so you can see the format — it is not a real customer, which is why it carries a burned-in label. To actually run this, film two or three real families on a phone. Fifteen minutes each.

The five questions

Where they
actually go.

Same five questions, three different places they can be asked. Which one you use is set by the campaign objective you pick in Ads Manager — it isn't a design choice.

Objective: Leads

Instant Form

Opens inside Facebook or Instagram. They never leave the app. Name and phone come pre-filled from their profile.

sabreconstructions.com.auBook a free site visit Learn more
↓ Questions appear here, in-app
  1. What are you looking to do? Knockdown rebuild / House raise / Reno
  2. Do you have plans drawn? Yes / Started / Not yet
  3. Where's the block? Suburb
  4. Budget Multiple choice band
  5. Mobile Pre-filled from their profile

Cheapest leads, lowest intent. They never see the page, the team or the licence.

Objective: Messages

Click to Message

Opens Messenger or WhatsApp with an automated greeting. Questions arrive as tappable buttons in the chat.

MessengerSend message Send
↓ Questions arrive as chat, one at a time
  1. "G'day — quick one so we can help properly."
  2. Rebuild, raise or reno? Three buttons
  3. Plans drawn? Yes / No buttons
  4. Suburb, then budget band
  5. Mobile, typed

Costs a bit more per lead, quality is higher. Someone who'll type in a chat is warmer.

Recommended

Landing page + popup

The ad sends them to one of the three pages below. The page builds trust, the popup does the qualifying.

sabreconstructions.com.auGet my free site visit Learn more
↓ Popup opens on the page, one question per screen
  1. What are you looking to do?
  2. Do you have plans drawn?
  3. Where's the block?
  4. Roughly what budget?
  5. Best mobile to reach you on?

Only two of the five need typing. Everything else is a tap. Leads email straight through.

⚠ Budget bands need confirming. The popup currently offers Under $300k / $300–450k / $450–650k / $650k+ / Still working it out. These are placeholders until Sabre confirms them against real job sizes — a bathroom reno and a knockdown rebuild aren't the same range.
